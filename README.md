# MedBridge

MedBridge is an informational tool that gives healthcare providers cultural and communication context when treating migrant patients in Switzerland. It does not diagnose, does not give medical advice and stores no patient data. The cultural and clinical content is a draft that has not been reviewed by a clinician. This is a student project in development.

The Translate tool sends the text you enter to a translation service. Nothing is saved. Do not write your name or personal details.

Provider accounts, community posts and votes are stored on a server. Patient pages store nothing and collect no personal data.

Reported posts may be reviewed by an automated system.
